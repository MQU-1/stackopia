Trust, by design

We can see your spending. We can never touch it.

Stackopia is built as a read-only companion to your existing bank accounts — never a place that holds your money.

🔒

Read-only, non-custodial

Bank connections are view-only. Stackopia is never able to move, withdraw, or hold your funds.

🔐

Encrypted end to end

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Account credentials are never stored on Stackopia's servers.

🚫

Your data isn't for sale

No selling data to advertisers, ever. Full account and data deletion, any time, in one tap.

⚖

Interest-free by design

No interest-bearing products. Saving tools are built around consistency, not lending.

⚠

Heads up: Stackopia is currently in pre-launch. Bank integrations, licensing, and regulatory review are in progress ahead of public launch.
